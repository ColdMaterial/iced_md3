# AI Usage Policy
This policy defines how outside contributors may use AI tools when contributing their work to Iced MD3. Project maintainers are not confined by this policy, as they have already gained the trust necessary to let them use AI at their discretion.

## Disclosure
All AI usage in any form must be disclosed. You must state the tool you used (e.g. Claude Code, Cursor, Amp), the AI model(s), along with the extent that the work was AI-assisted.

## Allowed content
Text and code are the only acceptable types of content. Submitting other media, such as images, audio or video to Iced MD3 is prohibited.

## Human-in-the-loop
LLMs tend to produce overly verbose output that is hard to parse through and, when it comes to code, often broken in subtle ways. We expect contributors to fully understand the work they submit to Iced MD3. If you can't understand the changes made by the LLM, and how they affect the bigger picture, do not contribute to this project.

## Communication and translation
Translation tools and LLMs may be used when interacting with project maintainers and other contributors. If you opt to use an LLM, make sure that its output is not overly verbose, and doesn't introduce any additional information to the translated text.
